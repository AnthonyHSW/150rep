Two things in that doc worth reading rather than skimming:

1. The refusal must be 422, not 403. BusynessService.validate routes every 401/403 to AuthService.handleRejection, which signs the user out — that's how the UCI domain rule reaches the app. A 403 geofence refusal would log people out of the app for reporting from home. The body needs the string outside_geofence; that's what the client matches on.
2. Required fields break older app builds (they'd 422 on every report), so backend and app build should land together — the doc notes the optional-then-required path if you need to stage it.

I didn't need the location data after all — I used the published ARC coordinates as you chose, overridable via ARC_LATITUDE/ARC_LONGITUDE on the backend. If you have surveyed coordinates for the building, change them in ArcGeofence.swift and the env vars together.

Also flagged in the doc: this proves the device reports a position at the ARC. A spoofed location still passes, since GPS is self-reported — the cheap hardening later is cross-checking a report against the reporter's own presence/active-workout row rather than trying to harden coordinates.
